# SniperCoders earned-link outreach brief

Backlinks cannot be created by code. This brief is for relevant editorial mentions earned after the content passes its quality gate. Never pay for followed links, use private blog networks, automate comments, or make a testimonial conditional on a link. Mark paid placements `rel="sponsored"`.

## Readiness

The topic pages are currently noindex drafts and excluded from the sitemap. Do not pitch a page until it is indexable, its copy and claims have been reviewed, and any downloadable asset works. The audience, angles and assets below are page-specific outreach hypotheses, not promises of coverage or referring domains.

## Page-by-page target and pitch map

Use the page's distinct buyer question as the pitch subject. The three pitch angles are: **A)** offer the page's practical framework as a useful reference; **B)** invite an editor to critique the page's decision checklist; **C)** propose a short expert contribution answering the page's specific buyer question. Use branded or naked-URL anchors most often; use partial-match anchors only when they read naturally.
