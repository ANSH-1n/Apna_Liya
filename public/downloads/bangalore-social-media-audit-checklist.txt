SNIPERCODERS | SOCIAL MEDIA AUDIT CHECKLIST

Use this worksheet to prepare for a social media strategy review. It is a planning aid, not a performance guarantee.

BUSINESS CONTEXT
[ ] Describe the offer in plain language.
[ ] Identify the audiences and the questions they ask before taking action.
[ ] Record the business objective this social programme should support.
[ ] Note any claims or topics that require legal or compliance review.

CHANNEL REVIEW
[ ] Give each active channel a clear role.
[ ] Check that profile descriptions, links and contact paths are current.
[ ] Identify formats the team can produce consistently.
[ ] Note where conversations need a timely human response.

CONTENT AND OPERATIONS
[ ] List recurring themes that explain the offer, process, expertise and customer questions.
[ ] Assign an owner for source material, review, approval and publishing.
[ ] Agree how urgent edits and corrections are handled.
[ ] Keep a record of assumptions that should be validated.

MEASUREMENT
[ ] Separate publishing activity from audience response.
[ ] Choose signals connected to the objective and available data.
[ ] Record what platform reporting cannot establish on its own.
[ ] Agree what the team will review and what decisions may follow.
